# Fund Switch prototype — functional flow updates

Period covered: **September 14–18, 2026**

These notes intentionally exclude visual styling and component-polish changes. They focus on scenarios, business-rule handling, and journey changes for BA and PM review.

## Entry and discovery

- Added the Login page as the main prototype entry point.
- Added the authenticated Dashboard step before the customer enters Services.
- Added a first-login Fund Switch introduction that can take the customer directly to Services.
- Connected Fund Switch entry points from both Services and the customer’s Investments holdings.

## Policy eligibility and duplicate-request handling

- Limited Fund Switch selection to eligible, in-force VUL policies.
- Kept lapsed and terminated policies visible for context but prevented customers from selecting them.
- Added a clear explanation for traditional or Non-VUL policies that do not contain switchable investment funds.
- Scoped the existing-request validation to the selected policy. A pending Fund Switch on one policy does not block a different eligible policy.
- Prevented a second Fund Switch request from being started for the same policy while its current request is still active.

## Fund availability and minimum rules

- Added product-specific target-fund availability instead of presenting one common fund list to every policy.
- Enforced same-currency switching: Peso policies only expose Peso funds, and US Dollar policies only expose US Dollar funds.
- Added the PHP 10,000 and USD 500 minimum-switch validations.
- Added boundary scenarios confirming that exactly PHP 10,000 or USD 500 is accepted, while values below the minimum are blocked.
- Changing the source fund now clears the previous target and suitability state so stale instructions cannot be submitted.

## Risk suitability flow

- Added conditional suitability handling: same-risk or lower-risk targets can continue without RPQ and IPS.
- Higher-risk targets now require the Risk Profile Questionnaire followed by Investment Policy Statement acknowledgement.
- A risk result below the selected target blocks continuation and provides recovery through answer review or choosing another fund.
- Completed RPQ answers and the calculated result are retained when only the target fund changes.
- Because IPS is target-specific, changing the target requires a fresh IPS acknowledgement and rechecks RPQ alignment against the new fund.

## Income Paying Fund special case

- Added a conditional multi-source flow when an Income Paying Fund is selected as the target.
- The customer can add held source funds until the combined allocation reaches exactly 100%.
- Continue remains unavailable until the full 100% allocation requirement is met.
- Added an insufficient-allocation scenario. If eligible source funds cannot total 100%, the switch is blocked and the customer is directed to choose another target fund.

## Draft, submission, and tracking

- Added Save as Draft and resume behavior, with the draft linked to the correct policy and journey position.
- Removed the policy-owner signature step because the request is submitted from an authenticated customer session.
- Submission now proceeds directly from review and confirmation to the request-success page.
- The success journey now returns the customer to Services, where My Requests remains the source for submitted and in-progress tracking.
- Removed submitted and in-progress indicators from Dashboard and Policy Details.
- Added post-processing behavior: Dashboard and Policy Details show a completion indicator only after the Fund Switch is completed, and Policy Details reflects the updated allocation.
