Frequently asked questions

We’re Here To Help You Understand

What is a VUL fund?expand_more

A VUL fund is an investment option within a variable unit-linked insurance plan. Your premium is allocated to funds based on your goals and risk profile.

How do I choose the right fund?expand_more

Start with your investment goal, time horizon, and comfort with market fluctuations. Our risk profiler can help you understand which options may suit you.

What is NAVPU?expand_more

Net Asset Value Per Unit reflects the value of one unit in a fund. It changes as the value of the fund’s underlying investments changes.

Can fund values go down?expand_more

Yes. Fund values may rise or fall depending on market conditions, and returns are not guaranteed.
